My name is Avery Stampp, I am 24 years old and I live in Connecticut. My current interests are are Reading and Writing. I have been trying to learn Russian for a couple months now (я плохой на рускйи). I like reading Russian literature and will write about it on this blog. But I do enjoy Dickens, Hemingway, and a whole host of other "classic" authors. I currently do not have a job but I used to be a longtime grocery store employee before I got burnt out and dismayed with trying to find meaning in life. I also used to work in a paint store and will probably write a boring article on some of the weird things that go into paint to make it... paint!

I am also someone that has dealt with a lot of anxiety and depression. I am not so interested in talking about it because it feels like every person and their pet turtle has something to say about it. But it mainly stemmed from a fear of death partnered with an existential crisis with work and goals, and maybe a little[^1] of drinking to avoid the things I needed to do in life. Oh well can't change the past can we.

Other than reading I really don't have hobbies, I'd say I like chess but if I play chess I get a sort of feeling I am turning into Bobby Fischer and will go crazy from studying the game to much, to the point where the only thing I can see when I close my eyes is the board and pieces moving. I'd say I like movies but admittedly I have not watched many. Same for TV I really haven't seen what everyone watches. I don't use social media (sorry [Zuck](https://i.redd.it/j7py871pa9wc1.jpeg)), nor do I embaress myself with dating apps. 

I guess you could say as for right now, I'm kind of a loser... But I do have some goals. Working on this blog of course is one but I also want to
- Live in my own place
- Study Eurasia/Russia
- Get a decent job that allows me to do both of those things

Maybe after those goals are complete I'm still a loser but who can say that in todays econmomy living on your own is losing in life? From time to time I will critique the world around me. In fact its really all I do outside reading, part of starting this blog is to allow me to write my critiques of the world in a manner that will hopefully make sense. Maybe even change me for the better.
